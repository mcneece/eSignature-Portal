Find Group Admin

Group Admins in Adobe Acrobat Sign are individuals who oversee a specific group of users. Using this tool, you can find your group admin and contact them to request access to their group, or to discuss changes that need to be made to the overall group settings.

You can find an admin by entering the name of an existing group, or by entering an active user's email. If you choose to search by email this will return that users' admin.
