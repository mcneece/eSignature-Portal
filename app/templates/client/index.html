Adobe Acrobat Sign

eSignature is a method of collecting a signature(s) on a document through electronic means, rather than physically sending & signing paper. To electronically sign a document, a signer navigates to a secure website and completes a series of acknowledgements meant to verify their identity and intent to sign the document(s).
